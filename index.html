            How to play

            pull back · aim · release
            ☝️
            Drag backward from anywhere on the course, like a slingshot. The farther you pull, the harder you hit. Let go to swing.
            1
            Power flies low and far, then rolls hot. Its preview stops at the first thing it hits. After that you're guessing.
            2
            Loft goes up and over trees, bumpers, blocks, water and lava. The preview shows where it lands, but not how far it rolls after.
            3
            Precision stays on the ground and has a short range. Its laser preview shows the whole path, including banks, rifts and moving parts.

            The course

            ⛱
            Sand stops a rolling ball fast. Ice barely slows it at all. Greens run a little quicker.
            💧
            Water costs +1 and lava costs +2. Either one sends you back to where you hit from.
            ⛰
            Mountains and the windmill are too tall to fly over. You can Loft over trees.
            »
            Boost arrows speed up a rolling ball. Springs launch it into the air. Rifts teleport it, keeping its speed. Bumpers bounce it back faster.
            ★
            Every hole hides one star. It doesn't change your score, just your bragging rights.
            ⛳
            A ball rolling too fast lips out of the cup. After par + 6 strokes you pick up and move on.
